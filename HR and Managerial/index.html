Interview-day checklist

  • Attend the PPT and write every EDG point you hear (10 of the 12 earlier reports say questions came straight from it). Those notes beat anything you read online.
  • Your 1-page cheat sheet: 90-second intro, 60-second MathWorks pitch, location + 2 reasons, favourite value + story, 2 strengths + 2 weaknesses, 4 questions to ask. For practice only: you can't use outside help during the rounds.
  • Keep a calm, steady pace; think aloud; ask for feedback at the end of each round (several seniors did).
  • Don't use GPT or any helper during the process (disqualification).
  • Between rounds, drink water and reset. The rounds come in random order, so a manager round might be first.
