Differences to remember

The GfG posts often include a group discussion and a longer round list. Your JAF lists none: test → PPT → technical → managerial → HR. But the IIIT Hyderabad 2026 EDG intern drive (CZ-IIITH26) did have a 10-person GD, so a GD is now a real possibility: prepare the 15-minute version on the HR page. Prepare for what your JAF says, and keep the GD ready just in case.
