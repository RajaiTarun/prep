How to use this plan
  • Pick today's day below. Each day has ONE theme and three study blocks (A, B, C), so there's no hourly subject switching.
  • Tick tasks as you finish them. Tasks marked Core are the must-dos.
  • Exam today? Turn on "Exam day" to see only Core tasks (about 3 hours). Skipped tasks go to the Day 8 buffer.
  • Every topic has its own Quiz button (usually 8-10 interview-level questions). Finish the topic, then press it. One question at a time; your best score is saved on the button.
  • Never skip the 9:30 PM Talk-aloud. 6-9:30 PM stays free for college exams and assignments.

Ticks and quiz scores are saved in this browser only.
