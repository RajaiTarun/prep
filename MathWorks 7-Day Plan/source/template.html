How to use this plan
  • Two tech chunks, one theme each: Chunk 1 (Sat 21 to Tue 24) = C++ + OOPS revision, your POSIX shell, OS, and C++ threading/concurrency. Chunk 2 (Wed 25 to Fri 27) = SOLID, design patterns, LLD, HLD, MerchFlow and the Kafka-style log. Sat 28 = non-tech final wrap-up + mocks.
  • Every day: N = 30-min non-tech slot at 8:30 AM (HR + Managerial, out loud), A, B, C = the day's chunk, D = 1-1.5 h DSA (only problems seniors were asked).
  • Tick tasks as you finish them. Tasks marked Core are the must-dos.
  • Exam today? Turn on "Exam day" to see only Core tasks (about 3 hours). Skipped tasks go to the Sat 28 buffer.
  • Every topic has its own Quiz button (usually 8-10 interview-level questions). Finish the topic, then press it. One question at a time; your best score is saved on the button.
  • Never skip the 8:30 AM non-tech slot (it is first so an assignment can't eat it). 6-9:30 PM stays free for college exams and assignments.

Ticks and quiz scores are saved in this browser only.
