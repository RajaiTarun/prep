experience 1 : 

I recently interviewed for the Software Engineer/EDG role at MathWorks. The process had an online assessment followed by technical and HR/managerial rounds.

The online test covered DSA, aptitude, OOP, OS, DBMS, and programming concepts, along with coding questions.

The technical interviews focused mainly on DSA, C/C++, core CS fundamentals, and projects mentioned on my resume. I was asked to explain my approach and optimize my solutions rather than just give the final answer.

The HR/managerial round was mostly about my projects, teamwork, strengths, weaknesses, and why I wanted to join MathWorks.

Overall, the interview was challenging but fair. I would recommend being strong in DSA, CS fundamentals, and knowing your resume/projects thoroughly.

experience 2 : 

Round 3: Technical Interview

The third round was the technical interview.

First, they asked me 4 coding questions. They were around easy to medium level.

After that, they asked me to write code for some OOPs concepts like virtual functions and the diamond problem.

They also asked some questions from OOPs and OS.

Round 4: Managerial Round

The fourth round was the managerial round.

Here, they mainly asked situational questions, such as:
How will you lead a team?
How will you handle a tight deadline?
How will you handle a problem within your team?
How will you manage different tasks?

The main thing is to explain how you would handle the situation logically and calmly.

Round 5: HR Round

The final round was the HR round.

They asked mainly behavioural questions like:
Why MathWorks?
What are your strengths?
What are your weaknesses?
Other general HR questions.
